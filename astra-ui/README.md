# Project Astra

*"Ad Astra."* — *To the stars.*

---

A universe is not born overnight.

It is shaped by stories, choices, and the people who leave their mark upon it.

Project Astra draws inspiration from many places.

From **Icarus**, who reminds us that even a fall can become a legend.

From **Odysseus**, who defied gods to find his way home.

From **Eraya**, where fortune favors those who continue forward despite uncertainty.

The rest...

Will reveal itself in time.

Every star has a purpose.

Every world has a story.

Every mystery deserves to be discovered.
